macOS

The first launch

Notewise is signed, but with the project's own certificate rather than an Apple Developer ID, so macOS cannot confirm who made it and stops the first launch. Once per download, it takes three steps.

  1. 1

    Put Notewise in Applications and open it from there. From the .dmg, drag it across; from the .zip or .app, move the unpacked Notewise.app.

  2. 2

    macOS says it could not verify that Notewise is free of malware. Choose Done.

  3. 3

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security, and choose Open Anyway beside the line about Notewise. It stays there for about an hour after the blocked attempt. Confirm with your login password.

Permissions you grant carry over to later releases, because every release is signed with the same certificate. To check that your copy is the one released on GitHub, run codesign -d -r- /Applications/Notewise.app and compare the certificate hash with the one in the release notes.

After it is running

Download a transcription model

Settings → Transcription models. large-v3-turbo is the one worth having: close to the largest model in accuracy, a fraction of the time, and it is what fixes most “it did not hear that properly” transcripts.

Point it at a model for summaries

Install Ollama and pull something (llama3.1:8b is a good start), and nothing leaves your machine. Or add your own API key for a hosted provider, and the app will tell you plainly that it is now sending transcripts there.

Turn on search by meaning

ollama pull nomic-embed-text, then Settings → Search by meaning → Build index. Embedding runs locally and never goes to your chat provider.

Something not working?

The app's About screen reports what your build can actually do: whether it can record, which model is loaded, and whether it runs locally. Most reports turn out to be one of those saying no.
